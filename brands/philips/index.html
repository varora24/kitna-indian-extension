Is Philips Indian?

43/100

No. Philips is a foreign brand. It is headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands.

Some of its India range is made here, and some is imported. Kitna Indian scores it 43/100, Mixed Origin.

Philips manufactures lighting and some personal care lines in India. Note that Philips-branded kitchen and grooming appliances are made by Versuni, a separate company controlled by Hillhouse Investment that licenses the Philips name, and many are imported from China.
